## 🍉🗡️ Math Fruit Cutter Game
An action-packed, educational arcade game that combines the satisfying mechanics of slicing flying fruit with practicing math equations and problem-solving!

## 🚀 Features
- **Arcade-Style Action:** Swipe or click to slice and cut fruits as they fly across the screen.
- **Math-Integrated Gameplay:** Match your cuts to the correct answers or target numbers to score points.
- **Dynamic Challenges:** Test your reflexes, speed, and mental math simultaneously.
- **Engaging Visuals & Effects:** Smooth slicing animations and score tracking.

## 🎮 How to Play
1. Start a new game session.
2. Read the math question or prompt presented on the screen.
3. Slice (click and drag / swipe) the fruit displaying the correct answer.
4. Avoid slicing the wrong fruits or missing targets to keep your streak and health/score up!
